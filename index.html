Real estate website prototypes

Four demonstration sites for Saudi real estate businesses, in English and Arabic. All brands, figures and licence numbers are sample content.

Dhilal CommunitiesA villa-community developer: home finder, community pages with live site plans, floor plans, design studio, Wafi payment plans and buyer guides. Sarh DevelopmentsA tower and mixed-use developer: interactive floor and unit picker, branded residences, office and retail leasing, investor relations. Nakhla ResidencesA luxury villa compound: villa pages, facilities, location and viewing requests. Sakan RealtyA brokerage: property search, map, calculators, owner listings and an agency dashboard. Demonstration only.
