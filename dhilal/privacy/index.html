Privacy notice

We process your personal data in line with Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL). This is illustrative text for a sample brand.

What we collect

The name, mobile number, email and contact preferences you enter in our forms, and the homes you save on your device.

Why

To answer your enquiry, arrange visits and send the information you asked for, based on your consent.

Your rights

You can access, correct or ask us to destroy your data and withdraw consent at any time by writing to privacy@dhilal.example.

Retention

Enquiry data is kept for no longer than 24 months unless you become a customer.

In this prototype no data is sent anywhere; form entries are stored on your device only.
