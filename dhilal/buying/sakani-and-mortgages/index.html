Sakani support and mortgages

How Sakani support and a bank mortgage work together when you buy a Dhilal home.

Who Sakani is for

Sakani is the Ministry of Municipalities and Housing's home-ownership programme for Saudi families. Eligibility and support amounts are set by the programme, so check your status on the Sakani platform first.

Using support with a mortgage

If you are eligible, your support can be combined with a mortgage from a partner bank. Our sales advisers coordinate the paperwork between you, the bank and the developer.

Typical mortgage terms

Saudi banks usually finance up to 90% for a first home, over terms of up to 25 or 30 years, with the instalment capped as a share of your salary. Use our calculator for a first estimate, then get a pre-approval.

Off-plan and mortgages

For off-plan homes, many banks finance the stage payments as construction progresses. Ask your adviser which partner banks offer this for each community.

General information, not legal or financial advice. Rules change; confirm current requirements with REGA, Sakani or your bank.
