Frequently asked questions

Is my off-plan payment safe?

Yes. Payments go into the project's Wafi escrow account and are released only against REGA-approved construction stages.

Can I visit before buying?

Every community has a sales centre or show home. Book a slot online or walk in during opening hours.

How long does a reservation last?

A refundable deposit holds your plot for 14 days while your contract is prepared.

Can I change the layout?

Within the design studio you choose finishes and options. Structural changes are not offered, so that every home stays within its approved permit.

What warranty do I get?

Structural elements carry a long-term warranty under Saudi law, and we add a 12-month snagging service and on-site maintenance.
