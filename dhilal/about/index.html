Neighbourhoods built for shade

Dhilal (ظلال, “shade”) began with a simple brief: build Saudi neighbourhoods where children can walk to the park and neighbours can sit outside in the evening.

We plan every community around a central park and shaded streets, design homes around the majlis and the courtyard, and protect buyers through Wafi-licensed escrow on every off-plan sale. This is a sample brand for demonstration.
