Piling complete at Sarh Tower

All 412 piles are in place, keeping the tower on schedule for a 2029 handover.

The foundation contractor has completed all 412 bored piles at Sarh Tower, and excavation for the second basement level is under way.

An independent engineer has certified the stage, and the related escrow release has been approved under the project's Wafi licence.

Buyers can follow progress on the project page, which is updated each month with photos.
