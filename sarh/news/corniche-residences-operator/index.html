Five-star operator signed for Corniche Residences

Owners will receive hotel housekeeping, dining and a managed rental programme.

Sarh has appointed a five-star hospitality operator to manage services at Corniche Residences in Jeddah.

Owners will be able to place their residence in a managed rental programme when they are away, with income reported monthly.

The operator's name will be announced at the public launch.
