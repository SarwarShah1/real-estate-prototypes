Overview

Sarh Tower rises 38 storeys above King Fahd Road, minutes from the financial district. One- to three-bedroom residences and six penthouses share a 24-hour lobby, a residents' club on level 4, a lap pool, separate ladies' spa and gym, and two levels of valet parking.
