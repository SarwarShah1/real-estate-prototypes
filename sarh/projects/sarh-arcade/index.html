Overview

The Sarh Arcade is a two-level, climate-shaded street of 28 shops, cafés and restaurants serving the tower's residents, the business district and evening visitors along King Fahd Road.

  • Shaded, cooled outdoor street
  • 650-space shared car park
  • Direct access from tower lobby and metro
